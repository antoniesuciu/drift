These general rules require actual dated expiry verification, rather than an unconditional Friday formula. [CME Chapter 353, rules 35301, 35302.C/G and 35303.A](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/353/353.pdf).

## Historical roll convention: important distinction

CME's March 2022 notice states that ES/MES lead-month rollover changed from the Thursday one week before expiry to Monday of expiry week, effective **June 2022**. March 2022 was the final roll under the old procedure. Consequently, March 10 is the rule-derived historical lead-month date under that old procedure, while **March 14 remains our deliberately fixed Monday project switch**. The adopted specification already treats that schedule as project policy; retain it across the study and record the distinction. A vendor continuous/lead-month symbol would not implement this policy reliably. [CME Globex notice, March 7 issue / March 10 notice date, 2022](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/electronic-trading/2022/03/20220307.html).

The project still selects June at 09:30 New York on March 14 and excludes that first switched cash session. Retain both contracts' surrounding Globex sessions, and use a single actual contract for all return endpoints on subsequent eligible dates. This is a research selection rule, not an exchange requirement to trade or roll positions.

## Timestamp and definition admission checks

Databento's OHLCV schema confirms that `ts_event` is the **inclusive interval start**, derived from trade `ts_recv`; absent trades produce no record. Raw minute buckets therefore remain `[T−1m,T)` for our endpoint at T. Preserve raw labels, shift to completion once, and keep the baseline/adverse availability as modeled end+1/end+60 seconds. A no-row interval is not independently proven no-trade evidence, and historical acquisition time is not strategy receipt time. [Databento OHLCV schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas/ohlcv).

Definition records separately expose capture `ts_recv`, matching-engine `ts_event`, action, raw symbol, publisher/ID, tick, currency, activation and expiry. Databento explicitly warns that activation/expiration may have **date-only precision**, publisher-dependent. Preserve declared precision and effective versions; corroborate exact last-trade bounds against CME. Unknown/sentinel activation cannot be replaced by a fixture date. A daily snapshot or legitimate definition update need not be byte-identical to the first record. Test actual available definition variants against the pinned loader before enabling real admission. [Databento definition schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas/instrument-definitions).

No public document proves our actual requested files' record counts, complete endpoint coverage, historical provider IDs, original publication/revision history or exact per-contract definition contents. Those remain explicit acquisition/admission evidence for gates 013/014. Public documentation verification is complete to the limits recorded above; **real-data admission remains pending**.
