# Drift — technical requirements

Authoritative requirement IDs and acceptance criteria. [DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md) records technology choices and [ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) owns milestone status; this document defines the target platform, not a claim that it exists.

## Scope

- **Core platform:** one modular Python application with three agent families (RULE, ML, LLM), historical research, reproducibility, shared portfolio and risk, and Alpaca paper execution.
- **Initial scope:** NVDA ordinary shares, Alpaca historical SIP data, Alpaca for paper execution (D-032 supersedes IBKR); crypto is deferred. NVDA is the first configuration, not a constant in shared modules: providers, instruments, strategies and execution adapters are replaceable through small validated interfaces, and each new configuration needs its own data, cost and permission evidence ([NVDA plan](docs/data/NVDA_DATA_PLAN.md)).
- **Current task (D-033):** collect/store/validate 2020–2025 within a 2 GiB archive budget while preserving at least 5 GiB free. Later years receive mechanical integrity checks only; strategy evaluation remains development-only and further backtesting is deferred.
- **Frozen research split:** development 2020–2022, validation 2023, holdout 2024–2025. ML/LLM model selection waits for evaluated rule research.
- **Hypotheses, not assumptions:** EXP-001 profitability, ML uplift, LLM usefulness, the other rule families and any deep-learning or RL benefit.
- The owner separately authorized the bounded Alpaca/IEX operational paper trial (D-032). It tests execution without promoting the historical SIP hypothesis; M2/M3/M5 research acceptance remains pending. These specifications alone authorize no acquisition, account access or orders.

Each requirement names the first milestone that verifies it and a measurable acceptance criterion.

| REQ-RESEARCH-001 | Each experiment records hypothesis, parameters, data/software versions, periods, seed, trial ledger, costs and limitations, including failed trials. | M3 pinned replay yields identical proposals and PnL within a declared numeric tolerance; result tables include gross/net, trades, drawdown, turnover, uncertainty and failures. |
| REQ-STRAT-001 | First rule-based experiment is the preregistered NVDA ITSM adaptation: split-neutral previous cash close→10:00 and 15:00→15:30 ET sign agreement, at most one trade per full eligible session, approximately 30-minute horizon. | M3 tests long/short/zero/disagreement, split actions, missing data, delayed execution and early-close abstention against [EXP-001](experiments/exp001_itsm/README.md). Short simulation/activation requires explicit borrow assumptions/permissions; neither a SPY paper nor earlier MES planning establishes NVDA alpha. |
| REQ-STRAT-002 | Every rule-based candidate use the same proposal contract and preregistration. ORB, VWAP/Bollinger mean reversion, short-term reversal and trend following are specified by REQ-STRAT-011–015 ([rule specifications](docs/STRATEGIES.md)); statistical arbitrage, volatility breakout and relative strength remain future work (REQ-STRAT-016). | M7 admits a candidate to combined evaluation only with its own causal tests, costs, isolated results and combined portfolio evaluation; there is no requirement to implement every example. |
| REQ-STRAT-003–010 | Withdrawn ([D-035](docs/DECISIONS.md)): candlestick pattern agents were removed. The IDs stay reserved. | — |
| REQ-STRAT-011 | Statistical rule agents (ITSM, ORB, mean reversion, reversal, trend) are separate RULE agents in `strategies/rule/statistical`, share the common proposal contract and one causal session state, and use only completed candles available at the decision. Every numeric threshold is a validated parameter; documented defaults are version-tagged research choices, never tuned or validated values. | Per model: hand-calculated fixtures, an independent exact-arithmetic differential test over generated sessions, future-candle invariance and parameter-validation cases, all offline. Mutating a boundary, edge-trigger or window rule must fail a test. |
| REQ-STRAT-012 | Opening range breakout: the range is the high/low of the first 5, 15 or 30 minutes from 09:30 New York; inside the entry window the first completed candle closing strictly beyond it that passes every enabled filter proposes LONG or SHORT; at most one proposal per session, with a NO_TRADE abstention at the cutoff. Optional filters: opening-range relative volume, VWAP alignment, breakout-candle volume multiple, prior true range below the n-session ATR. Exit by time horizon or session flatten, with optional stops. | Tests cover strict inequality, window edges, incomplete range, disabled direction, and each filter passing, failing and warming up. Catalyst selection and event-day filters stay unimplemented until their data exists. |
| REQ-STRAT-013 | Mean reversion in two variants sharing one edge-triggered fade: VWAP (z of the close against the session VWAP, using the volume-weighted dispersion of typical price) and Bollinger (z against a band of the last N same-session closes of an N-minute timeframe). A fresh crossing of ±k sigma inside a New York window is faded; the exit is a time horizon. Regime gates named by the research (macro calendar, ATR percentile) are not applied until their data and definition exist. | VWAP and Bollinger readings match an exact reference over generated sessions; crossings fire once, undefined readings (warm-up, zero dispersion, gaps) never fire; windows never span sessions. The research records unconditional fading as a losing proposition on trending instruments, so any historical result needs its own preregistration. |
| REQ-STRAT-014 | Short-term reversal: on each completed session-aligned bucket of 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, a negative close/open − 1 proposes LONG and a positive one SHORT when its magnitude reaches a configured threshold; the exit is a time horizon (15 minutes by default); a long/flat control disables the short side. | Tests cover direction, threshold equality, the control, other timeframes and gap buckets, plus an exact-reference differential test. |
| REQ-STRAT-015 | Trend following: a fresh cross of the fast simple moving average above the slow one over completed N-minute closes proposes LONG and below it SHORT; windows stay within one session and need slow + 1 bars; the position leaves on the opposite cross or the session flatten and does not reverse in the same step. The research names trend following without a rule, so this crossover is the lab's own definition. | Tests cover both crosses, equality semantics, warm-up, a disabled direction and an exact-reference differential test. |
| REQ-STRAT-016 | Relative strength/weakness, statistical arbitrage and pairs, and volatility breakout are future work. Relative strength needs benchmark or sector data and a multi-instrument contract; the others need multi-leg data or a compression definition. None is implemented or admitted until a separate task supplies data and definitions. | Recorded as deferred; no code path accepts them. |
| REQ-ML-001 | Future authorized ML research may support independent predictions and rule filtering through the common contracts. Model selection and training wait until rule-based research has been evaluated. | A separately authorized M4 task preregisters a simple baseline and net-cost comparison to rule/no-trade controls. Negative results complete research; neither this requirement nor feature reuse starts model selection now. |
| REQ-ML-002 | Features, labels, transforms and model selection respect chronological splits. Purge/embargo overlapping outcomes where needed. Record model/feature versions, calibration, class imbalance and drift. | M4 leakage tests show no fitting outside training folds or training label overlap with evaluation periods; inference artifacts reproduce predictions within declared tolerance. Deep learning/RL require a new justified experiment. |
| REQ-LLM-001 | Later LLM agents analyze news, earnings, macro/events and sentiment using licensed, timestamped source text; outputs are advisory with evidence and uncertainty. | M8 offline evaluation compares no-LLM/simple-text controls; excludes late text, records prompt/model/output versions, hallucinations, limitations and token/source costs. |
| REQ-LLM-002 | LLMs and external text cannot access broker credentials/tools, alter risk or issue authorized order intents. | M8 injection/malformed-output/stale-source tests cause rejection/abstention; audit confirms source text remains data and advisory tooling is allow-listed. |

## Simulation and statistical validation

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance evidence / first gate |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-BACKTEST-001 | Use a verified NautilusTrader release/API for event-driven historical simulation with explicit event ordering and realistic order lifecycle. | M1 offline import/engine smoke; M3 fixtures prove next-available-event fills, rejection and partial-fill accounting. No fills on information unavailable at decision time. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-002 | Model multiplier/currency, commissions, exchange/regulatory fees, spread, slippage and adverse cost scenarios. State bar-only fill limitations. | M3 hand-calculated long/short/flat PnL reconciles to simulated results; compare zero/base/stressed costs without spread double-counting. M5 assesses quote-aware sensitivity where material. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-004 | Run all registered strategies by default, or a selected subset, over a year or date range, concurrently within a configurable bound or sequentially; each strategy has independent state and capital (default $20,000), and a failure, crash or timeout costs only that strategy. Windows outside coverage fail; nothing substitutes data. A new strategy of any family needs a registry entry and no runner change. | Sequential and concurrent runs hash identically; exception, process death and timeout tests leave the others stored; a plugin strategy of another family runs unchanged; worker overlap is bounded; mutation checks fail these tests when the guarantee is removed. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-005 | Persist every order, fill, trade, proposal, per-bar equity and drawdown, metric and complete configuration in immutable Parquet with DuckDB views. A run id derives from its inputs and links strategy, portfolio, parameters, data and code versions; repeats are reused and disagreement refused; every attempt, including failures, is logged. | Hand-calculated run through stored tables; money reconciles across tables; a plain DuckDB client reads the same files; tampering and non-reproducible repeats are detected; a failed publish leaves nothing visible. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-006 | Persistent virtual portfolios, independent of broker accounts, hold capital, positions, equity, trades and versioned configuration, and advance by segments. A segmented portfolio equals one uninterrupted run; continuation is refused when that cannot be guaranteed (open position, order live across the boundary, non-replayable strategy, changed market history, unaccepted code change). Experiments stay separate. | Equivalence of trades, fills, equity and metrics for every strategy, including stateful configurations and sub-penny fills, and on the real development archive; gaps and overlaps cannot be requested; racing commits land once; every refusal condition is tested. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-007 | Backtests respect the frozen split: development open, validation by explicit recorded opt-in, holdout refused with no override, checked before data is read and again by the engine. Windows containing a split refuse positions that could cross it and any configuration that is not declared split-safe. | Interval, CLI and engine-level refusal tests, including a caller naming holdout as authorized; split-unsafe configurations and undeclared plugins are refused on a window with a split. |
| REQ-BACKTEST-003 | A backtest runner on the pinned NautilusTrader runs registered strategies on synthetic data, and the initial ITSM baseline on a verified immutable historical pilot dataset from a validated configuration (instrument metadata, capital, seeded data or a versioned historical dataset reference, explicit costs, sizing and risk limits with no defaults) and reports trades, metrics, a ledger-to-engine reconciliation and content hashes. A decision fills at the open of the first bar starting strictly after decision time plus order latency, adverse by configured spread and slippage. | Hand-calculated equity long/short and futures-multiplier trades, no-retrospective-fill and look-ahead invariance for every strategy, reconciliation to the cent, byte-identical reports and a stable results hash for the same inputs, and configuration rejection cases. Synthetic results validate software only; a small development pilot establishes no out-of-sample evidence. Version 2 historical configuration binds the dataset/feed/action lineage; version 1 remains synthetic. |
| REQ-VALID-001 | Preregister chronological train/validation/untouched holdout and walk-forward analysis; account for overlapping labels, serial dependence and all tuning attempts. | M5 dated protocol plus auditable fold/trial manifests; confidence intervals use justified dependence-aware methods; final holdout is evaluated once per frozen candidate. |
| REQ-VALID-002 | Compare no-trade, simple/rule and suitable randomized/time controls; test regime, corporate-action, cost and relevant instrument-specific sensitivity. Separate research completion from promotion. | M5 writes an evidence-based go/modify/reject verdict, including null/negative outcomes. Retuned candidates receive new trial IDs; optional CPCV/DSR need methodological justification. |

## Unresolved inputs

Do not invent capital, account eligibility, real-time entitlement, tax jurisdiction, fee tiers, borrow, risk limits, delivered coverage or ML/LLM benefit. Each is resolved at the relevant gate. The instrument/provider/broker choice is settled; Alpaca rights/access, actual Alpaca paper account/feed compatibility and measured coverage remain prerequisites. Preserve the frozen split above and preregister fill/latency/cost parameters before viewing EXP-001 results.
